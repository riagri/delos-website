Five recruitment myths.

  1. MythRecruiters just fill vacancies.

    TruthWe understand your business, not just the vacancy, and the impact the role will have now and in the future. We don't place just to fill a gap. We place to support the growth of your business and the candidate.

  2. MythRecruiters only care about the CV.

    TruthWe take the time to understand you properly, not just your CV. You are more than a two page list of your experience, and what you are looking for is more than what's written on a job description.

  3. MythRecruiters just pass your CV on.

    TruthWe take the time to prepare you for every interview, so you walk in confident.

  4. MythYou need experience to be a recruiter.

    TruthIf you don't have direct recruitment experience but you have the motivation and skill set to succeed, our trainee roles are made for you.

  5. MythRecruitment is just a stop gap before another sales career.

    TruthWhether your first step turns into a long term career depends entirely on how it's approached. We help you understand what recruitment actually involves and whether it's right for you.
